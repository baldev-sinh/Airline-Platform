Help and travel information

Answers to the questions we hear most. Can't find yours? Our care team is happy to help.

How booking and payment work
  • Choose a flight, a fare, your seats and any extras, then review the total.
  • When you continue to payment, we hold your seats for 20 minutes and take you to our secure payment partner, Razorpay.
  • The payment link is valid for about 15 minutes. Once payment succeeds, your booking is confirmed and an e-ticket is issued for every traveller.
  • If payment doesn't go through, nothing is charged and the seats are released.

You can always see the status in My trips.

Baggage allowance

Your cabin and checked baggage allowance depends on the fare you choose. It is shown on each fare while you book and in your trip details.

You can add extra checked bags during booking, usually for less than at the airport.

Changing or cancelling a booking
  • A booking that is still awaiting payment can be cancelled online at any time from My trips. Its seats are released immediately.
  • Paid, ticketed bookings are cancelled or refunded according to the fare rules. Please contact customer care and keep your booking reference handy.
Travel documents

Enter each traveller's name exactly as it appears on the ID they will travel with. Carry a valid government photo ID, plus a passport and any required visas for international flights.
